About Edulife IT School

Learning that builds confidence for life.

We are an English-focused primary school that treats technology as a normal part of childhood learning — not an add-on. Since 2015 we have been building a school where strong foundations, a confident voice and practical skill grow together.

Why choose Edulife?

A stronger start for a changing world.

Alongside the national curriculum, our own curriculum helps children build confidence, communication and practical skills from the earliest years.

01

National + own curriculum

Children receive the national curriculum plus additional skills designed around real confidence and future readiness.

02

Phonetics & public speaking

Daily practice helps children pronounce clearly, express ideas and speak in front of others with confidence.

03

AI, computers & coding

Technology is introduced early through age-appropriate computer, coding and responsible AI activities.

04

Classwork finished in class

Focused teaching and guided practice help children complete their classwork at school, reducing tutor dependence.

05

Small, caring guidance

Teachers know each learner closely and provide the encouragement, feedback and structure they need.

06

Character with capability

Curiosity, kindness, responsibility and practical ability grow together in everyday school life.

Our history

Ten years of steady steps.

Edulife grew from one small classroom into two campuses without ever changing what it was for.

1

2015 — Edulife begins

The first classroom opens in Khagrachari with a small founding team and a clear idea about confident learning.

2

2018 — English & phonetics curriculum

Daily speaking practice and phonics become central rather than occasional.

3

2021 — Technology joins the timetable

Computer and coding classes are introduced alongside the national curriculum.

4

2023 — Luxmichari Campus opens

A second campus brings the same model to more families in the district.

5

2026 — Innovation Lab & AI literacy

A dedicated lab and age-appropriate AI activities extend what children can build.
