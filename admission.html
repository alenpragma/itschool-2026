Common questions

Families usually ask.

Which classes do you admit?

We admit from Nursery through Class Five across both campuses — see the full list above.

Do children need prior computer experience?

No. Technology is introduced gradually and age-appropriately from the earliest years.

Is there an entrance test?

There is a gentle, age-appropriate assessment — not an exam — so we understand where your child is.

Can we visit before applying?

Yes, and we encourage it. Book a campus visit.
